Cleaning up noisy radar

A radar sees a maneuvering aircraft only through noisy dots. Watch a classic Kalman filter and a small neural network (a GRU) try to recover the true flight path from those dots.

Live replay

Which one wins?

Average position error in metres over 500 unseen tracks per scenario (lower is better). Click a scenario to replay it above.

Bars are capped at 700 m. A Kalman filter tuned for straight flight (q=0.1) is far worse than the raw radar in turns, which is why one fixed setting never wins everywhere. The GRU never saw these tracks during training.

How the GRU learns

A recorded training run, replayed. At epoch 0 the network is untrained and only gives its starting guess. Each epoch it sees more tracks and adjusts. Drag the slider or press play and watch the green line move toward the true path.
